Common questions

How do I earn reward points?

You earn 1 point for every Rs 100 you spend, multiplied by your tier. Points are added after check-out. See the Rewards page for the full details.

What is the cancellation policy?

Rooms can be cancelled free of charge until 48 hours before check-in. Events need a 50% deposit, which is not refundable.

How do I change my contact details?

Open your Profile and choose Edit Profile.
